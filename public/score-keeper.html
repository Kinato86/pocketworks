Score Keeper

Keeps score for the games we play at work and turns the results into per-sport Elo leaderboards, team standings and head-to-head rivalries.

React NativeExpoAndroidSQLite

What it does

  • Five sports and countingTable tennis including round-the-table, fussball, jeu de boules, shuffleboard, and darts in 301, 501 and Tactics.
  • Only finished games countRecord a match after it is over: sport, who stood on each side, what it was played to, and the score. An impossible score is refused with a reason.
  • LeaderboardsA per-sport Elo rating, doubles and triples pairings, and rivalries with the full head-to-head record.
  • HistoryEvery match is listed and can be opened for details, a note, or deletion.

Under the hood

Expo and React Native, local-only on Android with SQLite. The rating and validation logic is pure TypeScript tested with Vitest, and the database layer runs its tests on Node's own SQLite.
