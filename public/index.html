Motivate

A game-agnostic simulation engine for playtesting board games: scripted AI players run thousands of games, and a dashboard shows who wins, where the points come from, and how rule variants shift the balance.

A Dutch-language Android app built around a handwritten journal of 63 campsite visits. Every page was transcribed verbatim — nothing guessed, every doubt logged — and the rating dimensions were counted from the journal itself.
